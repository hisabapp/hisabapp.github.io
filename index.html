The ledger

What's inside

Every feature actually shipping in v1.2.0 — nothing here is planned or coming soon.

CASH

Accounts that can't go negative

Cash, bank, wallet or card — split your money across as many accounts as you actually use, each with a running balance the app will never let overdraw.

EXP / INC

Categorized expense & income

Log spending and earnings against categories that make sense to you, then search and filter your full history.

XFER

Transfers between your own accounts

Move money from Cash in Hand to your bank account without it ever counting as income or expense.

DEBT

Debt & lending tracker

One running balance per person — every Debit and Credit against them updates a real account automatically, with a full PDF ledger export.

SAVE

Savings goals

Set a target, contribute toward it, withdraw when you need to — the goal's progress adjusts either way.

RPT

Reports & export

Category breakdowns, month-over-month trends, and a calendar to browse any day — exportable as PDF or CSV whenever you need the paperwork.

LOCK

PIN & biometric app lock

Fingerprint or face unlock, with an auto-lock timer you set — HISAB locks itself behind whatever your phone already supports.

A note on privacy

Ads are the only thing that ever leaves this app. HISAB shows ads to stay free to use, and that's the extent of it — your balances, transactions, debts and savings goals are written to a local database on your phone and nowhere else.

No account to create, nothing to sync, nothing sitting on a server that could leak. Uninstall the app and every number in it is gone with it, because it was never anywhere but here.

Installing the APK

  1. Android blocks installs from outside the Play Store by default — when you open the downloaded file, allow "Install from this source" when prompted, then install normally.
  2. If the download finishes but seems to hang before installing, open Play Store → profile icon → Play Protect → settings gear, and turn off "Scan apps with Play Protect" before retrying.
